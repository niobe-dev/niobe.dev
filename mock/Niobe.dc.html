THE IDEA

Full speed. Full awareness.

Niobe shows its work as it goes: what it read, what it ruled out, what it decided and why. You see the whole picture at a glance, step in exactly where it matters, and let the rest run. Control comes from clarity — and clarity is built in.

01 / SEE THE WORK
Every read, edit and command, live

The session pane is a running record: files opened, greps run, diffs applied, tests executed. Glance at it, or let it run and read it later.

02 / SEE THE WHY
A decision log beside every diff

Every non-obvious choice is timestamped with its reason — what was reused, what was kept stable, what was deliberately skipped. Review the reasoning as quickly as the code.

03 / SEE THE TEAM
Parallel agents in one pane

Test-writers, reviewers, type-checkers and dev servers run side by side. You always know who's doing what, and what they're waiting on.

04 / SEE THE COST
Itemized, as it happens

Reads, edits, shell and thinking metered separately. Cache hits and wasted retries surfaced. Set a budget and Niobe pauses when it's reached.
